Existence of Solutions

The equation Ax=bAx=bAx=b has a solution iff bbb is a linear combination of the columns of AAA.

This four statements are equivalent :

  • For each bbb in Rm\R^mRm, the equation Ax=bAx=bAx=b has a solution
  • Each bbb in Rm\R^mRm is a linear combination of the columns of AAA
  • The columns of AAA span Rm\R^mRm
  • AAA has a pivot position in every row.
