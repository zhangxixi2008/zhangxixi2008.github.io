Rotation and Rolling

Angular Kinematics

  • Angular Variables:
    • Radians: θ\thetaθ
    • Displacement: Δθ\Delta\thetaΔθ
    • Velocity: ω\omegaω
    • Acceleration: α\alphaα

Constant Acceleration

For an object undergoing constant angular acceleration, the linear accelerations are:

  • Tangential acceleration: at=αra_t = \alpha rat​=αr
  • Centripetal acceleration: an=v2r=ω2ra_n = \frac{v^2}{r} = \omega^2 ran​=rv2​=ω2r

Rolling Motion

Rolling motion can be decomposed as a combination of Translation and Rotation.

  • Velocity of center of mass: vcom=ωRv_{\text{com}} = \omega Rvcom​=ωR

Torque

Torque is the rotational equivalent of force on an object.

τ⃗=r⃗×F⃗=rFsin⁡ϕ\vec{\tau} = \vec{r} \times \vec{F} = r F \sin \phi τ=r×F=rFsinϕ

  • Moment arm: r⃗\vec{r}r
  • Line of action: the line along the force vector F⃗\vec{F}F

Newton’s Second Law for Rotation

The net torque on a system is equal to the rate of change of its angular momentum:

τ⃗net=dL⃗dt=Iα\vec{\tau}_{\text{net}} = \frac{d\vec{L}}{dt} = I \alpha τnet​=dtdL​=Iα

    l⃗=r⃗×p⃗=m(r⃗×v⃗)\vec{l} = \vec{r} \times \vec{p} = m (\vec{r} \times \vec{v}) l=r×p​=m(r×v)

  • For a system:

    L⃗=∑l⃗=Iω\vec{L} = \sum \vec{l} = I \omega L=∑l=Iω

Conservation of Angular Momentum

Law of Conservation of Angular Momentum:
If the net external torque is zero (τnet=0\tau_{\text{net}} = 0τnet​=0), then the total angular momentum of the system is conserved:
