[Honors Physics] Chapter 18 Thermodynamics

Section 1 Temperature

The Zeroth Law of Thermodynamics

If bodies A and B are each in thermal equilibrium with a third body T, then A and B are in thermal equilibrium with each other.

The Triple point of water is the point in which solid ice, liquid water, and water vapor coexist in thermal equilibrium.

Constant-Volume Gas Thermometer

By raising and lowering reservoir R, the mercury level in the left arm of the U-tube can always be brought to the zero of the scale to keep the gas volume constant

Linear Expansion

Section 4 Absorption of Heat

Heat QQQ is energy that is transferred between a system and its environment because of a temperature difference between them.

The **heat capacity CCC ** of an object is the proportionality constant between the heat QQQ that the object absorbs or loses and the resulting temperature change ΔT\Delta TΔT of the object;

Q=CΔTQ=C\Delta T Q=CΔT

and ccc is the specific heat of the material making up the object.

Q=cmΔTQ=cm\Delta T Q=cmΔT

The amount of energy per unit mass that must be transferred as heat when a sample completely undergoes a phase change, but WITHOUT changing the sample’s temperature, is called the heat of transformation L. It is also known as the latent heat.

Q=LmQ=Lm Q=Lm

  • heat of vaporization (liquid to gas/boiling) : LvL_vLv​
  • heat of fusion (solid to liquid/melting) : LFL_FLF​
