The Linear Oscillator

A particle with mass mmm moves under the influence of a Hooke’s law restoring force given by F=−kxF=-kxF=−kx exhibits simple harmonic motion with:

ω=kmT=2πmk\omega = \sqrt{\frac k m}\\[6px] T=2\pi\sqrt{\frac m k} ω=mk​​T=2πkm​​
