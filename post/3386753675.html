Force and Emotion

  • Newton’s first law : If no force acts on a body, the body’s velocity cannot change; that is , the body cannot accelerate (Works in inertial reference frame)

  • Newton’s second law : The net force on a body is equal to the product of the body’s mass and its acceleration.

    Unit : N (Newton) =kg×m/s2=\text{kg}\times\text{m/s}^2=kg×m/s2

    Internal force is the force between two objects in the system.

    Remember to put the total force and direction (or the vector or even draw or figure) for you answer

Special Force

  • Gravitational force
  • Normal force
  • Friction and tension (a cord pulled taut)
  • Newton’s third law : When two bodies interact, the forces on the bodies from each other are always equal in magnitude and opposite in direction

  • AAA is the effective cross-sectional area of the body (the area taken perpendicular to the relative velocity)
