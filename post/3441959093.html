MICS Info Session

须江 ： Counsel of HKUST, 贝尔实验室,

AI computing requirement increase roughly 4.6 times a year.

US对China的制裁：

  • 2022 《芯片和科学法案》
  • 2023 美国禁止向中国出口H800
  • 2024 美国禁止台积电帮中国造AI芯片
  • 2025 美国禁止任何人使用华为昇腾芯片

Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) : 9% before

Chip is the foundation as information age. Without IC chip, no way to build information infrastructure. Chip is like the food in the nation.

**Chip is the cornerstone of Chinese Economy : **

  • 十四五：芯片是“事关过埃及安全和发展全局的基础核心领域”
  • 十五五：芯片是国家“战略必争领域”、AI首要攻关领域

US is Stopping Chinese Chips :

  • Weaponizing chip technology
  • CHIP4 alliance
  • Controlling EDA
  • Chip Act
  • Netherland and Japan

Modern Alchemy:

  • Made from sand
  • Just 3 steps : Design, fabrication, test

PERFECT Chip:

  • Performance
  • Energy efficiency
  • Reliability
  • Functionality
  • Extensibility
  • Cost
  • Testability

Cerebras WES-3 has about 4 trillion transistors, nevertheless the Milky Way has only about 100 billion stars

卡脖子：Talent, EDA, Fabrication

IC is such a young Technology :

  • 1947 贝尔实验室发明晶体管
  • 1958 Jack Kilby 在TI发明集成电路，获得诺贝尔奖。

电基芯片、光电融合芯片、量子/分子/生物芯片

微电子领域的4个方向：

  1. 器件与制造：先进制造工艺、传感器
  2. 电路与光路：毫米波雷达、激光雷达
  3. 体系结构与系统：CPU，GPU
  4. EDA电子设计自动化
