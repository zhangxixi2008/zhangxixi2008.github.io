Gravitation Force (Motion)

Gravitation is the tendency for bodies to attract each other.

Shell theorem

A uniform spherical shell of matter attracts a particle that is:

  • Outside the shell : all the shell’s mass were concentrated at its center
  • Inside the shell: no net gravitational force

Principle of superposition : Gravitational forces obey the principle of superposition.

Kepler’s Law

The motion of satellites, both natural and artificial, is governed by these laws:

  1. The law of obits: All planets move in elliptical orbits with the Sun at one focus.

Energy in Planetary Motion

U=−GMmrK=GMm2rU=-\frac{GMm}r\\[5px] K=\frac{GMm}{2r} U=−rGMm​K=2rGMm​
