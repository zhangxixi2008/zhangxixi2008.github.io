Xu Jiang: AI chip, Electronic-Photonic IC, EDA, MPSoC

3 components:

  1. Lecture
  2. Visit of lab
  3. Real chip factory

IC Industry :

  • Compound annual growth rate : 9%
  • US is blocking China from developing IC
    • CHIP4
  • Chinese Mainland is the Biggest IC Buyer

EDA

Various IC

Memory :

  • NAND : in USB key or labtop (turn off the power can still)
  • DRAM : Short memory, only need to remember data short(nearly micro second). Turn off the power, all the information is gone. But it is fast.

Logic:

  • GPUs : Main working for AI. To play action game. G stands for graphic, so it is dealing with graphic at first. Then to do video, and also good for AI.
  • CPUs : Process one task at a time.

Analog:

  • Signal : Proces signals. Deal with micro waves
  • Power : Providing power

SoCs : Put all together
