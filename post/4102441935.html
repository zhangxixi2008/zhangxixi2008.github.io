Continuity of the inverse function

What is inverse? It must be bijective.

For a continuous interval:

A continuous function on an interval is invertible iff it is strictly monotonic.

If f:[a,b]→Rf:[a,b]\to \Rf:[a,b]→R is strictly increasing and continuous, then fff is invertible, and its inverse f−1f^{-1}f−1 is also strictly increasing and continuous. (The same holds if fff is strictly decreasing and continuous)
