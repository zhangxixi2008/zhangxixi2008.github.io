fi,k=fi−1,k×10+si⋅gi,k（添加乘号）+fi−1,k−1⋅si（不添加乘号）f_{i,k} = f_{i-1,k} \times 10 + s_i \cdot g_{i,k}\text{（添加乘号）}+f_{i-1,k-1}\cdot s_i\text{（不添加乘号）} fi,k​=fi−1,k​×10+si​⋅gi,k​（添加乘号）+fi−1,k−1​⋅si​（不添加乘号）

矩阵+NTT优化

这种东西肯定是拿生成函数来优化的。

我们令：

Fi(x)=∑k=0nfi,k⋅xkF_i(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} f_{i,k} \cdot x^k Fi​(x)=k=0∑n​fi,k​⋅xk

Gi(x)=∑k=0ngi,k⋅xkG_i(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} g_{i,k} \cdot x^k Gi​(x)=k=0∑n​gi,k​⋅xk

则：

Fi(x)=10Fi−1(x)+siGi−1(x)F_i(x) = 10 F_{i-1}(x) + s_i G_{i-1}(x) Fi​(x)=10Fi−1​(x)+si​Gi−1​(x)

Gi(x)=Gi−1(x)+x⋅Fi−1(x)G_i(x) = G_{i-1}(x) + x \cdot F_{i-1}(x) Gi​(x)=Gi−1​(x)+x⋅Fi−1​(x)

故：

[Fi(x)Gi(x)]=[Fi−1(x)Gi−1(x)]×[10+sixxsi1]\begin{bmatrix} F_i(x) & G_i(x) \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} F_{i-1}(x) & G_{i-1}(x) \end{bmatrix} \times \begin{bmatrix} 10 + s_i x & x \\ s_i & 1 \end{bmatrix} [Fi​(x)​Gi​(x)​]=[Fi−1​(x)​Gi−1​(x)​]×[10+si​xsi​​x1​]

边界条件：

[F0(x)G0(x)]=[01]\begin{bmatrix} F_0(x) & G_0(x) \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} [F0​(x)​G0​(x)​]=[0​1​]

答案：

[xk]Fn(x)\boxed{ \begin{bmatrix} x^k \end{bmatrix} F_n(x) } [xk​]Fn​(x)​

令 Mi=[10+sixxsi1]M_i = \begin{bmatrix} 10 + s_i x & x \\ s_i & 1 \end{bmatrix}Mi​=[10+si​xsi​​x1​]，分治求 ∏i=1nMi\prod_{i=1}^{n} M_i∏i=1n​Mi​ 即可
